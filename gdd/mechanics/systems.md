# Vision
- You can peek around corners
- sight isn't necessarily symmetrical
# Offense
- Different weapons have different effective ranges, sound levels
- Some defenses are impervious to some weapons
# Movement
- Walking is slower and quieter than running
# Physical
- Walls and such can be destroyed
- you can carry weight-appropriate things, but will be slowed down
# Information
- Agents will notice when things move, disappear, etc.
- Agents will notice the absence or death of other agents
- Agents will identify where you are, what you're holding, etc.
# Relational
- You can talk to, bribe, etc. non-aggressive agents
- Agents can be aware of things you've done in the past
# Elemental
- Fire spreads
- Water puts out fire
- Water conducts electricity
- Water can be frozen; ice can be melted
- Electronics can be EMPed
- Biological things can be poisoned and such
